I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at [UNC-Chapel Hill](https://www.unc.edu/) working with [Prof. Gedas Bertasius](https://www.gedasbertasius.com/). Previously, I obtained my master's degree from [Brown University](https://www.brown.edu/) in 2023, advised by [Prof. Chen Sun](https://chensun.me/). Before that, I obtained my bachelor's degree from [Southeast University](https://www.seu.edu.cn/english/) in China in 2020.

During my Ph.D., I interned at Meta FAIR (summer 2026, fall 2024, summer 2024) and at Google DeepMind (summer 2025).

I'm broadly interested in computer vision and multimodal AI, particularly **long-video understanding and reasoning**. My research focuses on developing intelligent agents that can efficiently perceive, search, and reason over long videos. Recently, I have been especially interested in **agentic video understanding, long-term multimodal memory, and adaptive information retrieval**, aiming to enable AI systems to effectively understand and reason over long-context multimodal information.

I like music, guitar, basketball, and PC games.
